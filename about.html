OUR STORY

A marketplace built around the buyer.

CarBreezy started with a simple frustration: buying a car in Pakistan usually means juggling half a dozen sites, shady listings, and zero transparency about what you're actually paying for.

So we built a marketplace that puts clear information first — real specifications, upfront pricing, and a simple path from browsing to booking an inspection. Whether you're looking for a new family sedan or a reliable used hatchback, we want the decision to feel less like a gamble and more like a choice.
